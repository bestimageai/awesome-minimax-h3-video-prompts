> Turn useful tutorials, model reviews and API integrations into referral earnings with the [bestimage.ai Affiliate Program](https://bestimage.ai/affiliate-program/).

## Current program terms

- **20%** on a referred user's first valid paid order.
- **10%** on subsequent valid paid orders made within **60 days after that user registers**.
- Create referral links and manage referral activity in the affiliate workspace.

Order eligibility and payouts follow the [current affiliate agreement](https://bestimage.ai/affiliate-agreement/).

Only eligible and correctly attributed orders qualify. Refunds, chargebacks, risk-cancelled orders, policy abuse, and other exclusions are reviewed under the active affiliate policy. Participation does not guarantee traffic, orders, commissions, or income.

## How to join

1. Create or sign in to a regular [bestimage.ai](https://bestimage.ai) account.
2. Open the [affiliate program](https://bestimage.ai/affiliate-program/), complete the affiliate profile, and accept the [current agreement](https://bestimage.ai/affiliate-agreement/).
3. Create a referral link containing your assigned `via` value.
4. Share the link with a clear affiliate disclosure and monitor eligible referrals, commissions, and payout settings in the affiliate workspace.

## Responsible promotion

- Clearly disclose that you may receive a commission when someone purchases through your link.
- Describe prices, models, features, availability, and results accurately; verify time-sensitive claims before publishing.
- Do not promise guaranteed earnings, results, or performance.
- Do not use misleading ads, spam, self-referrals, attribution manipulation, or unauthorized brand assets.
- Follow applicable law, platform rules, and the current bestimage.ai affiliate agreement.
