# Contribution quality and media rights

The **bestimage.ai team** maintains this prompt library for practical audiovisual creation.

## Writing a useful recipe

Define a concrete deliverable, input roles, action sequence, camera direction, sound, continuity constraints and review criteria. Use clear, adjustable details so readers can adapt the scene to their own work.

## Rights and attribution

Submit original or appropriately licensed material. Preserve any attribution required by its license, and obtain permission for identifiable people, voices, music, brands and reference media.

The repository's license terms are in [LICENSE](../LICENSE). Model weights, third-party videos, trademarks and user-supplied media have their own applicable terms.

## Images and examples

The [image notes](../assets/README.md) provide the generation tool, prompt and scene mapping for each illustration. The [official H3 gallery](./official-h3-examples.md) links to MiniMax's published examples with their original credits.

## Reviewing a contribution

Include the complete prompt, model or tool, input roles, settings and output when available. Review the result for timing, geometry, speaker consistency, continuity and technical accuracy. Support factual model claims with official documentation.

For submission steps, see the [contribution guide](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
