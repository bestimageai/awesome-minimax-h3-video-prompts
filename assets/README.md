## Generation record

- **Generation date:** 2026-08-28.
- **Generation tool:** Codex built-in `image_gen`.
- **Inputs:** initial generation used text only. IMG-006 was subsequently edited using its own generated first output as the reference.
- **Exact generation briefs:** [nine submitted prompts](./minimax-h3-reference-image-prompts.md).
- **Post-processing:** no conventional retouching or format conversion. One built-in image edit removed incidental brand-like marks from IMG-006; its exact edit prompt is recorded with the image brief. Final tool-output PNGs were copied unchanged into this repository.
- **Role:** visual navigation, mood concepts and possible starting references. A scene still is not a complete reference pack: additional product views, motion clips or authorized voice material requested by a video recipe are not bundled.

## Visual review and limitations

All nine outputs were visually inspected for the requested subjects, object counts, composition and visible text or watermarks. The product image contains one box, two trays and one lid; the creator image retains three separate accessories; the tile study contains three tiles in the specified color order; the dialogue scene contains two hedgehogs with distinct scarves.

- The cover is an editorial montage, not a continuous scene or a scientific diagram. Its wave-tank panel must not be used to teach propagation or apparatus construction.
- The separate wave-tank image is a proposed setup. No measured result or physically validated animation is supplied.
- The athlete is a fictional motion reference, not a qualified demonstration of training technique; court markings and equipment details need professional review before instructional use.
- The kiosk, products, performer and characters are fictional concepts, not endorsements or representations of real services.
- Reference fidelity, contact, geometry, motion, exact dialogue and audio synchronization must be checked again after any video generation.
- The externally referenced bestimage.ai logo in the README is the site's actual brand asset, not a generated image and not included in the nine-image count.
- Official MiniMax GIF/MP4 examples remain attributed external links in the [official gallery](../docs/official-h3-examples.md); they are separate from these nine assets.

## Rules for future assets

- Submit only media you have the right to share and preserve applicable source credits.
- Record the exact tool/model when available, input roles, prompts, date, edits and known defects.
- Keep documentation images in this directory using descriptive filenames and relative links.
- Do not remove required authorship or license notices, copy promotional watermarks, or disguise third-party results as this project's output.
- Do not claim a tested model result without its actual generation record.
